Over this summer, I have done more activities than in past years. This includes rock and boulder climbing. I never imagined myself doing this as I’m not an active person. I have done yoga, running, and other flexibility exercises. I haven’t done any strength-related activities, so rock climbing pushed me in that direction. I was scared at first when starting because of my slight fear of heights. It took a while for me to get used to it. My friend, who introduced me to climbing, suggested I start with boulder climbing. The height is less intimidating, but the routes are more difficult. I prefer bouldering because I am more comfortable climbing up and down.

However, I still find myself rock climbing to push myself with my fear of heights. After a while, my friends and I decided to go to Nevada Climbing Center (NCC). This gym is different from the UNLV Student Recreation and Wellness Center (SRWC). I felt more confident and comfortable with the rock wall there. It’s a lot shorter and beginner-friendly. The boulder wall is a different case. It’s quite a bit more difficult compared to the SRWC boulder wall. Even my friends, who are more experienced than me, had a hard time climbing it. But we love a challenge. Talking and helping each other to complete routes are my favorite moments in these gyms. That’s what I love about climbing; the community is so welcoming and supportive. I felt encouraged to keep climbing because of my friends and strangers who were very supportive. Climbing is a recent favorite hobby of mine, and it will not go away anytime soon.
