Summer Climbing

UNLV Student Recreation and Wellness has many services and events for all students and community members. They have many programs, including the Outdoor Adventures program. This program hosts outdoor trips, activites and their bike shop.There is one thing OA is known for is its climbing walls. Right now, SRWC has one of the tallest rock walls in the Nevada area (roughly 43 feet high). The program hosts many events and activities at their walls. Building community among beginners and professionals alike. More information can be found on their website.
